# Vietnamese Speech Annotation Rubric

## 1. Transcription Accuracy

### Objective

Determine whether the transcript accurately represents the understandable speech in the audio.

### Good

- Spoken words are represented.
- No unsupported words are added.
- Informal speech is preserved.
- Spontaneous speech is not rewritten into polished prose.

### Bad

- Words are omitted.
- Words are invented.
- Spoken grammar is silently corrected.
- Important speech events are removed.
