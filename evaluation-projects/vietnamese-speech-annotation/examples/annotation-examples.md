# Vietnamese Speech Annotation Examples

This document demonstrates how the annotation framework can be applied to common spontaneous-speech situations.

## Example 1 — Filler Word

### Audio Observation

The speaker says:

> "Ừm, tôi nghĩ chúng ta nên đi."

### Correct Annotation

```text
[ừm] tôi nghĩ chúng ta nên đi.
