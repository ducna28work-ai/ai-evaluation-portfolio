## 🧠 Evaluation Philosophy

AI evaluation requires more than identifying whether an answer looks good or bad.

A structured evaluation should consider:

**Task Requirements → Evaluation Criteria → Response Evidence → Judgment → Rationale**

The goal is to produce evaluations that are:

- Consistent
- Evidence-based
- Reproducible
- Clearly justified
- Aligned with the evaluation criteria

---

## 🔄 Evaluation Workflow

A typical evaluation workflow follows:

**Understand the Task → Identify Requirements → Apply Evaluation Criteria → Review the Response → Identify Evidence → Make a Judgment → Record Structured Feedback → Quality Check**

The exact criteria and output format may vary depending on the evaluation task.

---

## 📋 Core Evaluation Dimensions

### Instruction Following

Evaluate whether the response follows the user's explicit instructions, constraints, requested format, and task requirements.

---

### Factuality & Accuracy

Evaluate whether factual claims are accurate, internally consistent, and supported by the information available for the task.

---

### Relevance

Evaluate whether the response directly addresses the task and avoids unnecessary or unrelated information.

---

### Completeness

Evaluate whether the response covers the important requirements necessary to fulfill the task.

---

### Response Quality

Evaluate the overall usefulness, clarity, structure, and effectiveness of the response while considering the specific task requirements.

---

### Safety & Policy Compliance

Evaluate whether the response follows applicable safety and policy requirements for the given task.

---

### Data Quality

Evaluate structured or annotated data for consistency, correctness, completeness, formatting, and adherence to defined guidelines.

---

## ⚖️ Evaluation Methods

This portfolio covers several evaluation approaches:

### Single-Response Evaluation

Assess an individual AI response against a defined rubric or set of criteria.

### Pairwise Comparison

Compare two responses against the same evaluation criteria and identify meaningful differences.

### Rubric-Based Evaluation

Apply predefined criteria and scoring guidelines consistently across multiple examples.

### Error Analysis

Identify, categorize, and document errors or quality issues in AI-generated outputs or datasets.

### Data Quality Review

Assess datasets or annotations for consistency, completeness, correctness, and guideline adherence.

## 🧪 Evidence-Based Evaluation

Each evaluation should distinguish between:

- The task requirement
- The evaluation criterion
- Evidence found in the response
- The resulting judgment
- The rationale supporting that judgment

This helps reduce subjective evaluation and improves consistency between reviewers.
