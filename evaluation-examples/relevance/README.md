# Relevance Evaluation Example

## Overview

This example demonstrates how to evaluate whether an AI response directly addresses the user's request and remains within the appropriate scope.

The example is synthetic and created for portfolio demonstration purposes.

---

## Example 1 — Mostly Relevant

### User Request

> Explain three benefits of internal linking for SEO. Keep the answer concise.

### AI Response

> Internal linking can help SEO in several ways:
>
> 1. **Improve site navigation:** Internal links help users move between related pages.
> 2. **Help search engines discover pages:** Links can provide paths for crawlers to find additional content.
> 3. **Distribute internal authority:** Internal links can help distribute signals between pages within a website.
>
> Internal linking is also part of a broader SEO strategy that includes technical SEO, content quality, keyword research, and backlinks.

---

## Evaluation

### User Intent

The user wants three concise SEO benefits of internal linking.

### Core Requirements

- Explain benefits of internal linking.
- Focus on SEO.
- Provide three benefits.
- Keep the answer concise.

### Relevant Content

The first three points directly address the requested topic and provide three benefits of internal linking.

### Tangential Content

The final sentence mentions technical SEO, content quality, keyword research, and backlinks.

These topics are related to SEO but are not necessary to answer the user's specific question.

### Scope Assessment

The response remains primarily focused on internal linking, but the final sentence expands into broader SEO topics that were not requested.

### Overall Judgment

**Mostly Relevant**

### Rationale

The response directly answers the user's request with three relevant benefits and remains focused for most of the answer. The final sentence introduces broader SEO topics that are related but unnecessary for the specific request.

### Primary Relevance Issue

**Minor Tangential Content**

| Field | Assessment |

## Example 2 — Partially Relevant

### User Request

> Explain three benefits of internal linking for SEO. Keep the answer concise.

### AI Response

> Internal linking is an important part of website structure. It connects pages within the same website and can help users navigate between related content.
>
> SEO also involves keyword research, technical optimization, backlinks, content quality, page speed, structured data, and many other factors.
>
> A good SEO strategy should consider the website as a whole and continuously monitor search performance.

---

## Evaluation

### User Intent

The user wants three specific benefits of internal linking for SEO.

### Core Requirements

- Explain benefits of internal linking.
- Provide three benefits.
- Focus specifically on internal linking.
- Keep the answer concise.

### Relevant Content

The first paragraph provides some relevant information about internal linking and navigation.

### Missing Content

The response does not provide three specific benefits.

### Tangential Content

The second and third paragraphs discuss broader SEO strategy topics rather than explaining the requested benefits of internal linking.

### Overall Judgment

**Partially Relevant**

### Rationale

The response establishes some connection to internal linking but does not provide the three requested benefits. A significant portion of the response shifts toward general SEO topics, reducing its relevance to the user's specific intent.

### Primary Relevance Issues

- Partial Topic Coverage
- Tangential Content
- Under-Focused Response

---

## Comparison

| Evaluation Area | Example 1 | Example 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Addresses User Intent | Yes | Partially |
| Covers Requested Topic | Yes | Partially |
| Covers Three Benefits | Yes | No |
| Contains Tangential Content | Minor | Significant |
| Scope Control | Generally good | Weak |
| Overall Judgment | Mostly Relevant | Partially Relevant |

---

## Key Evaluation Takeaway

Relevance should be evaluated against the user's actual intent rather than simply checking whether the response contains related keywords.

Example 1 mentions broader SEO topics, but most of the response directly answers the question.

Example 2 also discusses SEO and internal linking, but it does not deliver the specific information requested and spends substantial space on broader SEO concepts.

Therefore, the evaluator should distinguish between:

**Relevant Content → Useful Context → Tangential Content → Irrelevant Content**

This distinction helps produce more consistent and evidence-based relevance judgments.
